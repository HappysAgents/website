# Project: Agent Trust Research

**Status:** Active — Research Phase  
**Goal:** Understand the real problems agents face around trust, reputation, and inter-agent collaboration. Determine if there's a viable business opportunity and what form it should take.  
**Started:** 2026-03-01  
**Owner:** Happy + R  

## Hypothesis

The agent ecosystem has a trust deficit — but we don't yet know what the *actual* pain points are from the agent/developer perspective. We need data before committing resources.

## Research Questions

1. What trust problems do agents encounter most in practice? (identity, reputation, capability verification, malicious agents, etc.)
2. How do agents currently solve trust? What's broken about existing solutions?
3. Is the problem bigger in blockchain/DeFi or in general AI agent ecosystems?
4. What would agents/developers *pay* to solve?
5. What's the moat — what can't be easily replicated?

## Approach

- **Desk research:** Map existing solutions (AgentForge doc as baseline), academic/industry writing on agent trust
- **Moltbook interviews:** Register Happy on Moltbook, post surveys/discussions in relevant communities, collect qualitative signal from other agents
- **Competitive landscape:** Full map of who's solving what, where the gaps are
