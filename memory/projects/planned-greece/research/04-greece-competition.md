# Greece Home Maintenance — Competitive Intelligence Report
*Researched: 2026-03-05 | By: Happy (Chief of Staff)*

---

## TL;DR

- **Zero subscription-based preventive home maintenance companies exist in Greece.** The closest analogue (ARPM) bundles maintenance into full property management for overseas owners — nobody is selling a Planned-style standalone maintenance subscription to resident homeowners.
- **The market is fragmented**: reactive handymen (€30/h), a dominant on-demand marketplace (Douleutaras, ~Angi equivalent), and 10+ Airbnb management companies — but no one is connecting these into a proactive, structured care model for the property itself.
- **Labour is cheap by Western standards** (electricians ~€17/h, plumbers ~€14/h), meaning a Planned-style service could charge €150–250/month and capture substantial margin while being genuinely affordable to the target customer.

---

## 1. Subscription / Preventive Maintenance Services

**Finding: None exist in Greece targeting residential homeowners.**

After extensive search — including Greek-language queries for "συντήρηση σπιτιού πακέτο" (home maintenance package), "συνδρομή συντήρηση" (maintenance subscription) — zero companies were found offering a recurring preventive home maintenance subscription to individual homeowners in Greece.

The concept is entirely absent from the Greek market. The closest found:

### ARPM — Athens Riviera Property Management (arpm.gr)
- **What it is:** Full property management for overseas/expat owners. Annual fee model.
- **Services included:** Annual contents insurance (up to €100K), keyholding, **"planned preventative maintenance"**, monthly cleaning, mail collection, utility bill management.
- **Target customer:** Non-resident property owners (overseas Greeks, Golden Visa investors, expats)
- **Pricing:** Annual fee model — exact figures not published, requires consultation
- **Positioning:** "As available in other European countries and the US" — explicitly frames themselves as bringing Western PM standards to Greece
- **Gap:** They manage the whole property; they don't offer maintenance-only. You have to hire them for everything. Not scalable for resident homeowners who don't need full PM.

### Greece-Invest (greece-invest.gr)
- Property maintenance services including rental management, bookkeeping, utility payments, and maintenance — targeting foreign investors. Not a maintenance subscription — more of a concierge/admin service.

**Conclusion: The subscription preventive maintenance niche is 100% open.**

## 3. Handyman / Multi-Trade Services

### Hive Services (hiveservices.gr) ⭐ Closest competitor
- **What it is:** Athens-based handyman service. "All home services in 1 place."
- **Services:** Fixing, securing, hanging, lifting, installing, removing. AC maintenance, furniture assembly, baby proofing, painting, storage organization.
- **Pricing: €30/hour, €60 minimum charge.** Transparent, published pricing.
- **Note:** Hive is "a Keybee company" — Keybee is the dominant STR management company in Greece. Hive appears to be their in-house maintenance arm spun out or made accessible to general public.
- **Gap:** On-demand only. No preventive maintenance. No subscription model. No inspection reports.

### Handyguy (handyguy.gr)
- **What it is:** Athens premium handyman. Claims 20+ years experience in Greece and international cities (London, Paris, St. Moritz, New York). Positions as "trusted partner in home maintenance and care."
- **Pricing:** Not published. Positioned as a premium service.
- **"Good delivery promise"** — shows up with all tools needed, focuses on reliability and efficiency.
- **Gap:** Reactive. No subscription. No preventive inspection model visible. Small/boutique operation.

### Episkeuazo.gr
- **What it is:** Online directory/booking for emergency tradespeople — plumbers, electricians, Athens.
- **Pricing:** Market rate, quote-based. Plumbers, electricians available 24h.
- **Gap:** Pure reactive emergency service. Zero preventive angle.

### Ilektrologoi24.gr
- **What it is:** Electricians Athens 24h service. From €25 for a service call.
- **Positioning:** Budget emergency electricians.

**Summary of handyman market:** Fragmented, reactive, no dominant branded service. Pricing from €25–30/hour for standard work. No one offering systematic preventive maintenance.

## 4. Home Services Platforms (Greek Angi/HomeAdvisor)

### Douleutaras.gr / YourHero ⭐ Most important platform
- **What it is:** THE #1 home services marketplace in Greece and Cyprus. On-demand booking for 90+ categories of professionals.
- **Coverage:** Athens, Thessaloniki + Cyprus (Nicosia, Larnaca, Limassol, Paphos). Expanded to Ireland (YourPro.ie) and Portugal (YourHero.pt).
- **Business model:** Commission on completed jobs.
- **Scale:** 1,500+ vetted professionals. Has iOS app.
- **Funding:** Multiple rounds — €1M seed, then €1.8M Series A+ (2018 data). Total ~€3M+.
- **Gap:** Purely reactive, on-demand marketplace. No preventive care. No subscription. No property health record. You hire someone when something breaks — identical to Thumbtack/TaskRabbit in the US.

### Douleutaras category example: Electricians Athens
- 1,500+ professionals listed
- 24/7 availability
- Transparent pricing and reviews
- Book in 3 minutes

**This is the key structural insight:** Douleutaras validated that Greeks will pay for on-demand home services online. But no one has layered a subscription/preventive model on top of this demand. Douleutaras itself has no incentive to — they earn commission per job, so preventive maintenance that *reduces* emergencies would hurt their revenue.

**Industry pricing standard:** Management fees 15–25% of booking revenue, covering guest comms, cleaning, and basic maintenance. Higher-end services (24/7 concierge, professional photography, compliance) push toward 25%+.

**What maintenance they handle:** Reactive repairs only. When a guest reports a broken AC or leaky tap, the company dispatches a tradesperson. **None offer preventive/scheduled property care**. The AC just breaks. The water heater just fails. Then they scramble.

**Critical regulatory context:** Greece introduced new short-term rental regulations in 2024:
- Mandatory professional licensing for owners renting 2+ properties
- Strict health and safety requirements
- Athens introduced a 1-year moratorium on new STR registrations (Jan 2025)
- Properties in certain zones face restrictions

This regulation wave is **pushing STR hosts toward professionalization** — exactly the moment when a preventive maintenance product becomes essential for compliance.

From salary survey data (2024/2025):

| Trade | Annual Salary | Implied Hourly (full-time) | Market Charge Rate |
|-------|--------------|--------------------------|-------------------|
| Electrician | €30,600–35,700 | €15–17/h | €25–50/h (service call) |
| Plumber | €29,100 | €14/h | €30–60/h |
| HVAC Technician | €34,500 | €17/h | €35–60/h |
| General Handyman | ~€26,000 | €12–13/h | €30/h (Hive pricing) |

**Key insight:** Hive charges €30/hour to customers. Labour cost is ~€14–17/hour. That's a **gross margin of ~43–53% on labour** before overhead. A well-run maintenance subscription business could price services at €150–250/month and still deliver strong margins.

**Call-out fees observed in market:**
- Ilektrologoi24.gr: from €25 for a service call (budget end)
- Hive: €60 minimum charge
- Emergency plumber (24h): quotes available — typically €80–150/visit

## Top 3 Market Gaps

### Gap 1: Preventive/Proactive Maintenance — The White Space
**Nobody is doing this.** Every service is reactive. Homeowners call when something breaks. No company offers scheduled quarterly/annual walkthroughs, system checks, photo reports, or preventive care plans. The concept doesn't exist in the Greek market vocabulary. A company that introduces this framing has zero competition and can define the category.

### Gap 2: English-Language, Trust-Branded Service for Expat Owners
Greece has 380,000+ Golden Visa applicants (5th most issued in EU) and a large English-speaking expat population concentrated in Athens. These owners are cash-rich, time-poor, geographically distant from their properties, and deeply uncomfortable with the opaque, informal Greek trades market. ARPM targets this but bundles maintenance into full PM. A standalone, English-first, transparent-pricing maintenance service would win this segment by default. Nobody else is speaking directly to this customer in their language with their expectations.

### Gap 3: Data-Driven Property Health Record
No company in Greece gives homeowners a digital record of their property's maintenance history — what was done, when, photos before/after, what's due next. Every repair is one-off, cash-in-hand, no paper trail. This is a product layer no one has built. It creates lock-in, enables insurance conversations, and builds resale value documentation.

## Competitive Moat Opportunities

A new entrant could do things incumbents structurally cannot:

1. **Own the preventive maintenance category** — Douleutaras built a marketplace (reactive). Property managers manage tenants (not the property). Handymen wait for the phone to ring. A subscription preventive maintenance company is a new category, not a competitor to any of them. First-mover in a category is the most durable moat.

2. **Build the property health database** — Each visit becomes data. Over time, a company doing 500 quarterly visits has an unmatched dataset on how Greek apartments age and fail. This enables AI-powered maintenance predictions, insurance partnerships, and pricing power no handyman can match.

3. **Partner with the Airbnb managers, not compete with them** — The Keybees and ArisHosts of the world *need* a reliable preventive maintenance provider. They currently use ad-hoc local tradespeople. Offer a B2B maintenance subscription for property managers' portfolios — they become your distribution channel. They can't build this in-house without abandoning their core model.

4. **Professionalize a fragmented, informal market** — Greek tradespeople largely operate cash, no contracts, no accountability. A branded service with uniforms, digital booking, post-visit reports, and annual subscriptions signals quality that the informal market can never credibly claim. Trust is the ultimate moat in a market where trust is the primary gap.

5. **Ride the regulatory wave** — STR regulations (2024–2025) are forcing Athens Airbnb hosts to formalize. Health and safety requirements mean they *must* maintain properties to documented standards. A maintenance subscription with documentation output (inspection reports, photo evidence) directly addresses this compliance need. Incumbents don't offer documentation — they're trying to maximize booking revenue, not property health.
