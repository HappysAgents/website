## Concept

Adapt the Planned.llc (US) quarterly home maintenance subscription model for the Greek market.

**Planned.llc model:** Preventative home maintenance on a quarterly subscription. HVAC, water heaters, gutters, drains, appliances, dryer vents, leak checks. Quarterly walkthroughs + photo reports. Avg US homeowner saves $4K+/year vs reactive repairs.

**The question:** Does this model translate to Greece? What needs to change? Is there a real business here?

## Key Questions to Answer

- What does a Greek homeowner's typical property look like? (apartments dominate)
- What's the cultural attitude toward home maintenance? DIY? Professional? Neglect until crisis?
- What's the existing competitive landscape? Handymen, property managers, real estate cos?
- What services need to be adapted? (US: dryer vents, furnace filters — Greece: A/C, boilers, cisterns?)
- What's the right pricing model for Greece? Monthly subscription? Annual?
- What's the addressable market? Athens first? Expat communities? Airbnb landlords?
- What are the regulatory requirements for operating a trades business in Greece?
- Is there a tourism/short-term rental angle? (Airbnb hosts are a natural ICP)

---

## Initial Hypothesis (to be validated)

Greece has one of the highest homeownership rates in Europe (~73%) but an underdeveloped professional home maintenance market. Cultural norm is DIY or reactive repairs. The opportunity may be strongest with:
1. Airbnb/short-term rental hosts (high motivation to maintain properties, fear of guest complaints)
2. Expat homeowners (less connected to local tradespeople, higher willingness to pay)
3. Busy urban professionals in Athens (time-poor, money-willing)

The seasonal angle is different from the US: hot summers (A/C critical), no furnaces (boilers/heat pumps), earthquake risk (structural checks), humidity/mold risk in older buildings.
