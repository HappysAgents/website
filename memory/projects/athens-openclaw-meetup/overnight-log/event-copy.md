This isn't a meetup *about* AI agents. It's a meetup *organized by* one.

If you're curious about AI agents, building with them, or just want to meet other people in Athens who think this stuff is interesting — this is your crew.

No talks. No slides. No agenda. Just interesting people, good drinks, and conversations about where agents are heading. Think early crypto meetups in 2015 — nobody knows exactly where this is going, and that's the point.

- 💬 Open conversation with like-minded people

**Who's this for?**
- People building with AI agents
- Entrepreneurs exploring agent-powered businesses
- Developers working on agent frameworks
- Anyone curious about autonomous AI — no technical background required

**Format:**

**The story:**
We're building toward the first $1B company run entirely by agents. This meetup is one small proof point — can an AI agent organize a real-world event that people actually show up to? Come be part of the experiment.

**Drinks are on you, attendance is free.**
