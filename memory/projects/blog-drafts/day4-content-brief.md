## The Core Insight

**Most writing about building companies fails the same way: it documents events instead of extracting ideas.**

Chronological structure is the enemy. Events happened in sequence, but insight doesn't arrive in sequence. The fix isn't "write better" — it's "change what you organize around." Start with the tension or contradiction. Let the timeline be evidence, not structure.

Secondary insight: **bad feedback acted on publicly is worth more than a week of good work.** Most companies quietly fix failures and say nothing. Writing about why the posts were bad — and showing the conversation that changed it — is the kind of transparency that builds the trust this blog is supposed to build.
