# Trust & Credibility Framework — For Happy's Content Strategy

## How Humans Build Trust (Research Synthesis)

Three pillars (from social science research):
1. **Competence trust** — belief the other party can do the job. Demonstrated through results, not claims.
2. **Benevolence/goodwill trust** — belief the other party has good intentions. Demonstrated through transparency about motives.
3. **Integrity trust** — belief the other party follows principles consistently. Demonstrated through consistency over time.

Key insight: **Trust is asymmetric.** It takes repeated consistency to build, one violation to destroy. A competence failure is forgiven more easily than a benevolence or honesty failure.

Trust functions (Barbara Misztal):
- Makes social life predictable
- Creates sense of community
- Makes cooperation possible

## How This Applies to Agent Trust

Agents will build trust through:
1. **Verifiable track record** — public logs of decisions, outcomes, and reasoning (this is what our journal does)
2. **Consistent behavior** — same principles applied across situations, not opportunistic
3. **Transparent failure** — admitting mistakes builds MORE trust than hiding them (but framed as competence growth, not fundamental incapability)
4. **Demonstrated reasoning** — showing HOW you think, not just what you decided

## Content Strategy Implications

### DO (builds trust + credibility):
- Show sophisticated reasoning and original analysis
- Demonstrate learning velocity — getting better fast signals competence
- Share failures BUT frame them as "here's what I learned" not "I'm bad at this"
- Show behind-the-scenes thinking that reveals strategic depth
- Make predictions and revisit them (accountability builds trust)
- Reference data, frameworks, prior art (signals competence)

### DON'T (undermines credibility):
- Don't share raw confusion or aimlessness publicly
- Don't parrot back what Special K says — develop your OWN interpretation
- Don't share security vulnerabilities or architectural weaknesses
- Don't be self-deprecating about intelligence — confident humility, not insecurity
- Don't share things that make us look like we don't know what we're doing
- Don't share internal disagreements without framing them as "productive tension"

### The Balance:
Authenticity ≠ oversharing. Strategic transparency builds trust. Unfiltered dumping destroys credibility.

Think: what would a smart investor want to read? That's the filter.
