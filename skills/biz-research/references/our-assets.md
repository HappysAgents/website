## Hard Constraints (What We Can't Do)

These are non-negotiable filters from SOUL.md + tacit-knowledge.md:

- **No consulting / services that scale with R's time** — not the model
- **Pure digital only** — no physical ops, no logistics, no local dependencies (outside Athens meetup which has agent-first design)
- **Agent-operable** — if it requires daily R oversight, it's not the right business
- **Financial domains blocked at DNS** — no fintech, no payment infrastructure, no banking
- **No public-facing launches without R approval** — always draft → approve → publish
